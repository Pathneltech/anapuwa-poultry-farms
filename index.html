Who We Are

Anapuwa Poultry Farms — Rooted in Kwale

Founded over eight years ago in the heart of Kwale, Delta State, Anapuwa Poultry Farms has grown from a backyard passion into one of the most trusted poultry operations in the Niger Delta region.

We raise our birds with premium feed, clean water, and proper veterinary care — giving you the freshest, healthiest poultry products at fair prices, delivered right to your doorstep.

🌿

Natural Feeding

All birds fed with quality grains and nutritional supplements — no harmful additives.

Life at Anapuwa Farms

How We Work

From Hatch to Your Hands

01

Sourcing & Hatching

We source certified day-old chicks from trusted hatcheries and begin our carefully monitored brooding process.

02

Feeding & Health

Birds are fed premium quality feed with daily water supply, regular vaccinations, and vet supervision.

03

Growth & Monitoring

Each bird is weighed and monitored weekly. We ensure optimal housing conditions and ventilation.

04

Harvest & Delivery

Birds are harvested at market weight, hygienically dressed if needed, and delivered fresh to you.
